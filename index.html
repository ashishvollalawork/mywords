CHINNU...

Naa heart lo unna maatalu

♥

Chinnu… nuv ante naaku kopam ledu, hatred em ledu. Nijam cheppali ante naaku naa meede kopam vastundi. Nenu ninnu inthaga enduku nammanaa, inthaga enduku love chesanaa ani ippudu nannu nene question chesukuntunna.

Ninnu naa mardal ani, naa person ani, naa life lo last person ani anukunna. Love ani cheppi nannu namminchavu. Nuvve ala chesthe, inka vere vallani ela trust cheyali? Adi naaku just betrayal la anipinchaledu, naa whole heart ni break chesinattu anipinchindi.

Nuv em chesavo, em jarigindo naaku telusu. Kani nee meeda unna prema mundu avi anni pakkana petti, “ponile, naa mardal kada” ani malli nee daggariki vachaa. Nenu ninnu odileyali ani eppudu korukoledu. Nuv ade repeat chesina kuda, nee meeda unna prema tho ninnu accept chesaa.

Naa prema ento, nenu entha genuine-ga unnaano oka roju neeku poorthiga ardham avutundani wait chesaa.

“Naaku vere evaru dorakaka nee daggara unna” ani annavu. Kani nenu nuvve last ani fix ayipoyaa, Chinnu. Anduke naaku vere option avasaram ledu. Evaru unna, evaru vachina, naaku nuvve kaavali ani anukunna. “Nuv naa daggara unnaav” ani cheppinappudu, nenu adi words-ga kaadu, naa life lo biggest truth-ga nammaa.

Kani ippudu alochisthe, nenu ninnu maatrame love cheyaledu. Ninnu naa life lo oka part-ga maatrame chesukoledu. Naa life motham neeku ichesaa. Anduke ippudu pain kuda antha ekkuvaga undi.

A REALIZATION

Unconditional love ante ento...

Ippati varaku maa Amma ki oka gift ivvaledu. Chinna saree kuda koni ivvaledu. Naaku job vachina tarvatha first time neeku watch konnaa, dress konnaa. Enduku ante nuv happy-ga undaali ani. Nee face lo oka small smile kosam kuda chaala things chesaa.

Monna maa Amma ki saree konichaa. Aa saree chusi thanu happy-ga unna face chusina moment lo naaku oka vishayam ardham ayyindi.

Amma nenu em expect chesthunnaano ani adagaledu. Nenu em isthe adi chaalu ani happy ayyindi.

Appudu ninnu gurthu chesukunna. Enduku ante nenu ninnu kuda alane love chesaa. Nuv naaku em return istaav ani alochinchakunda, nuv nannu entha love chesthunnaav ani count cheyakunda, nuv naa pakkana unte chaalu ani anukunna.

WHAT HURT THE MOST

Nuv break chesindi naa heart maatrame kaadu.

Nuv break chesindi ninnu blindly nammina oka manishi trust.

Nenu ninnu naa family, friends, andari kanna mundu nammaa. Nuv cheppina maatani question cheyakunda nammaa. Nuv naa person ani blind-ga nammaa. Ade person naa trust ni break chesinappudu, aa pain ni ela explain cheyalo naaku kuda teliyatledu.

Nuv nannu mosam chesaav ani cheppadam easy. Kani nenu ninnu nammina way ni nuv break chesaav ani cheppadam chaala painful. Ippudu aa trust ni malli ela build cheyalo naaku teliyadu.

THE TRUTH I CANNOT HIDE

Naaku nee meeda kopam kanna, ippatiki prema ekkuva undi.

Ninnu marchipovali ani anukunna prathi sari, naa heart “thanu kaavali” ani aapestundi.

Ninnu vadileyali ani anukunna prathi sari, mana memories anni munduku vastunnayi.

Nuv naaku pain ichaav ani telusu. Kani ninnu vadileyadam kuda naaku pain-ey.

Ninnu punish cheyadaniki kaadu.

Ninnu blame cheyadaniki kaadu.

Naa love ni prove cheyadaniki kuda kaadu.

Just… nenu ninnu inka love chesthunna kabatti.

ALL I EVER ASKED

Nenu ninnu perfect-ga undamani eppudu korukoledu.

  • Naa daggara honest-ga, genuine-ga undaali ani korukunna.
  • Naa love ni value cheyyaali ani korukunna.
  • Naa trust ni break cheyyakudadhu ani korukunna.

Ippudu kuda naa heart lo nuvve unnaav. Kani aa heart lo ippudu oka bhayam kuda undi...
